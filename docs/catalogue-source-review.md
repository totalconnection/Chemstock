# Catalogue source review

Checked against Chemstock’s public WordPress sitemap and the September 2022 public brochure during the website rebuild.

## Imported records

- 33 canonical product pages with the product name and CAS number shown on Chemstock’s existing website.
- 251 additional named products or commercial grades from the public Chemstock specialty-chemicals brochure.
- Brochure-only records intentionally show chemical identity, current grade, specification, documents, source, and availability as **on request**. A CAS number, formula, stock position, certification, or technical limit was not inferred.

## Legacy pages excluded as duplicate or corrupt

- `/sodium-bbenzoate/` duplicates the sodium benzoate record and contains unrelated copied template content. It redirects to the canonical sodium benzoate page.
- `/p-tert-bbutylbenzoic-aacid/` contains unrelated copied template content. It redirects to the canonical p-tert-butylbenzoic acid page.
- `/triple_pressed_stearic_acid/` contains unrelated 911P/template content rather than a usable triple-pressed stearic acid record. It redirects to the canonical stearic acid page pending a verified grade specification.

## Brochure text held for manual confirmation

The PDF itself contains isolated or incomplete labels: `Monoammonium`, `Phosphate`, `Sodium`, `Sodium Acid`, and `Tetrasodium`. These were not published as standalone catalogue records because the intended chemical names cannot be established from the source.

Commercial or grouped entries such as `DHIN Plasticizer`, `Dicapryl Adipate / Sebacate`, mixed anhydrides, resin grades, brominated flame retardants, and trade-name products remain explicitly source-specific. Their composition and equivalence require supplier documentation.
